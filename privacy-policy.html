Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Knockout Roofs ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and how it's handled.

What We Collect

When you submit our inspection request form, we collect the information you provide directly:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Property address
  • Service you're interested in

We do not use cookies, tracking pixels for advertising, or collect information beyond what you submit through the form, aside from standard, anonymous website analytics (page views, general location by city/region, device type) used to understand how visitors use the site.

How We Use It

We use the information you submit solely to respond to your inspection request, follow up by phone, text, or email, and provide you a quote or schedule service. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Where It's Stored

Form submissions are stored in a private Google Sheet accessible only to Knockout Roofs staff. We take reasonable steps to keep this information secure, but no method of storage or transmission over the internet is 100% guaranteed.

Communication Consent

By submitting the form, you consent to Knockout Roofs contacting you by phone, text, or email regarding your inquiry. Message and data rates may apply to text communications. You can ask to stop receiving communications from us at any time by replying STOP to a text or emailing us directly.

Your Choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information we have on file for you at any time by contacting us using the details below.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your information? Reach us at info@knockoutroofs.com or 732-630-9446.

This policy covers general use of this website in plain language. It is not a substitute for review by a licensed attorney, particularly before running paid advertising (Google and Meta both require compliant privacy policies) or sending marketing text messages, which fall under separate legal requirements (TCPA).
